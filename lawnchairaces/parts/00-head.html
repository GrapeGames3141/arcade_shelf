How to fly

Staying up

Your chair hangs under 28 big helium balloons. They lift a little less the higher you go, so the chair settles at a float height. The altimeter on the left shows that height with a hollow marker.

  • Space pours water out of your ballast jugs. The chair gets lighter and climbs to a new float height.
  • E (hold) lets one balloon go. You sink about 40 m. Shooting one of your own balloons does the same thing.
  • Each balloon an opponent pops drops you about as much. If the float height drops below zero you are sinking: pour water fast, or you will splash into the lake and be out.
  • Helium slowly seeps through latex. You won't notice it for the first few minutes, but in a long match everyone gets heavier.

Steering

You can't steer a balloon, but the wind blows a different way at each height. The arrows on the altimeter show which way each layer is going, relative to where you're looking. Climb or sink to catch the layer that carries you where you want to go. Near the shore a breeze pushes back towards the middle. Drift past the trees and you're out.

For a nudge, hold W. The leaf blower strapped to the back of your chair swings you round to face where you look and pushes you that way. It overheats after about four seconds and has to cool before it starts again. Watch the BLOWER bar.

The rifle

  • It's a multi-pump pellet rifle. Hold R to pump it up to 10 times. More pumps mean a faster, flatter shot, but each pump is harder than the last.
  • After a shot, the next R works the bolt to load a pellet.
  • Right mouse looks through the 4× scope. Shift holds your breath to steady it. The scope dots are 5 milliradians apart, about 0.5 m at 100 m.
  • Pellets drop and drift with the wind. Aim high at long range. The drop guide (if on) puts a ring where your pellet will be when it reaches the range of the chair you're aiming at.
  • Hit a balloon to pop it. Hit a water jug to hole it: it leaks, and that chair climbs. Hit a balloon's string and it floats away.

Modes

In Duel and Free-for-all, the last chair still in the air wins. Target Practice gives you three minutes against balloons tethered to buoys, with more points for longer shots.
