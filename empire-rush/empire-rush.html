EMPIRE RUSH

Raise a clan from cave to town. Keep the fires lit. Burn your rival's hall.
  • Gather food, wood, stone and gold with your people. Build dwellings to grow the clan.
  • Build faster with more hands: every worker on a building speeds it up. Select it and press Add a builder, or send workers to it.
  • Changed your mind? Esc or right-click stops placing a building. Select a building that is still going up and press Cancel (or Delete) to get its resources back.
  • Every dawn your people eat. Run out of food and they starve. Plant fields early: they never run out, and in winter they only grow slowly.
  • At night wolves hunt anyone outside the firelight. Build braziers and lookouts, or ring the bell to shelter in the Hearth Hall. Every fifth night is a Blood Moon.
  • Advance from the Ember Age (cavemen) through the Kiln Age to the Forge Age, unlocking bowhunters, outriders and stonehurlers.
  • Win by burning the rival Hearth Hall. Lose yours and it is over.
