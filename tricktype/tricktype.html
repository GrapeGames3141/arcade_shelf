How to ride

A 90-second score attack. Your keyboard is your board.

1
Pick your trick by typing it

Each obstacle rolls in with three words. short is safe, medium scores more, long is the big trick. The first letter you type locks your choice. Backspace swaps.

2
Finish before you get there

The timer under the cards shows how far away it is. Reach the obstacle with the word unfinished and you bail. No typos means a PERFECT landing worth 1.5×.

3
Keep the line alive

Every trick adds +1 to your line multiplier. After you land, start the next trick before the bar drains, or type the MANUAL word to hold it. The line banks when the bar runs out. Bail and you lose the whole thing.

4
Slow-mo, Special, S-K-A-T-E

Big airs and grinds slow down time. Type the grab or switch-up for extra style. Perfect landings fill SPECIAL, which turns your big option into a gold trick. Land big tricks through floating letters to spell S-K-A-T-E.
