IN THE ROOM
1 · BUILD

You have a minute. Spend your budget on hay, wood and stone and hide your golden bell somewhere off the ground. Pieces drop onto whatever is below and hold still while you build; the shake test shows what would fall. Use honey to glue a piece to everything it touches, so roofs and overhangs hold until a big blast tears them loose.

2 · SIEGE

Take turns pulling back the catapult and launching sheep. They explode a moment after they land, and a blast sets off any sheep nearby, each bigger than the last.

3 · WIN

Knock the other bell to the ground to win the round. Out of sheep? Most damage wins. Best of three rounds.
