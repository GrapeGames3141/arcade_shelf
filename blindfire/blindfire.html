THE IDEA

You can't see your rivals. Fire anyway. The closer you land, the more you earn. Spend it on bigger blasts. Last launcher standing wins.

CLUES

Every landing is marked Cold → Scorching. A flash shows roughly where a rival fired from.

DANGER

Your own blast kills you. The aim ring turns red when you're inside it. Forest hides your launches.

THREE MODES

Drive, look at the map, or upgrade, one at a time. The match keeps going while you're in the map or the upgrades, so be quick: any driving key takes you straight back. The map opens where you left it.

UPGRADES

Eleven of them in four groups: bigger and cluster warheads, faster reloads and rockets, armor, repairs, smoke to hide your launches, radar, a spotter drone, and bounty contracts that raise every payout. Each level costs more than the last.
