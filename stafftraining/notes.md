# Staff Training — design notes

Idea (user, 2026-11-26): "a game where you're a cat training your human. Heavy on the humor." Then: title **Staff Training**, a human **couple**, build it.
The user asked not to push until they have played it.

What the couple adds: two awake staff back each other up, so you separate them; one can be charmed into defending you from the other;
they compare notes about whether you have been fed; they take turns at door duty; a cat on a lap pins that person in place; and the bed has two edges.

Staff: Marcus, tall, heavy sleeper, soft touch, works at the desk. Dana, light sleeper, holds out, owns the spray bottle, best lap.
The cat is Biscuit (four coats to pick from). The manual calls the staff CAN OPENER and THE LAP.

Decisions made without asking (change freely):
- Third-person chase camera at cat height, so the staff tower over you. Cartoon look with ink lines (the Doorbusters renderer).
- Controls follow the other moving games: floating left-thumb joystick (up walks, sideways turns), buttons on the right.
- Six lessons in one apartment, each 3 minutes or less, graded on a report card. Lessons unlock in order; the menu has UNLOCK ALL.
- No real brands, generic apartment. The humans speak in gibberish blips with speech bubbles; the cat's side is the manual's narration.

Ideas not built yet (ask before adding):
- Lesson 7, "Staff Do Not Take Vacations": sit in the suitcase, hide the passports, make them miss the taxi.
- The treat-bag lure ("pspsps") that drags your joystick toward the staff, costing dignity if you come when called.
- Gifts: carry the toy mouse and leave it on a pillow or in a shoe.
- Unrolling the toilet paper. The faucet. The vacuum as a boss. A dog next door. A guest who "isn't a cat person".
- A free-roam day with no objectives.
- Arcade lobby card (`assets/arcade/stafftraining.webp` + the `index.html` article) and the CLAUDE.md / README rows: waiting on the user's play-test.
