# Alley Knights — design notes

Origin (2026-11-23): brainstorm in Cartastrophe's vein. Squirrels → raccoons: a raccoon using a dumpster lid as a
shield and ridiculous trash weapons, fighting for control of the alley by running off the other varmints. The
win mechanism chosen was "eviction meters + dumpsters pay for upgrades" (option 4 built on 3): each faction has a
nerve bar; break them all for a clean sweep, otherwise survive to dawn; food from held dumpsters buys upgrades.
A shopping cart is a temporary mount that wears out. Name changed to Alley Knights; levels are "Nights".

The shield is a round galvanized trash-can lid (a real dumpster lid is 1.8 m wide, bigger than the raccoon).

Ideas not built yet: lid throw, garbage-truck sweep hazard, motion-sensor light that dazzles, more alleys
(laundromat, fish market), versus mode (dawn tally of dumpster time), raccoon customization (bandana/helmet).
Balance was tuned with the autopilot; needs a human pass, especially den raid speed (`DEN_MAX`, raid rate) and nerve.
