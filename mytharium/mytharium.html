How to look after the Mytharium
MouseDrag to orbit · right-drag or Shift-drag to pan · wheel zooms toward the cursor · double-click to fly to a spot · click a creature to pet it · press and hold a creature to pick it up and carry it
TouchDrag to orbit · pinch to zoom · two fingers to pan · double-tap to fly to a spot · tap a creature to pet it · hold to pick it up
KeysW/S zoom · A/D pan · Q/E rotate · R/C up/down · 1–9 views · F fullscreen · H hide interface · P photo mode · J journal · M sound
CareMist the plants (humidity keeps the moss green and the ferns from wilting; mist in bright light makes a rainbow) · the heat lamp warms the dragon's slate · tap the glass and everyone looks at you · wipe the fog off the front glass
FeedClover and apple for the unicorns, nectar and honey for the faeries, sea grapes for the mermaid, crickets and glowing coal for the dragon
NightFaeries glow, horns shine, mushrooms light up, will-o'-wisps drift over the pond; at dusk the dragon lights the lanterns and the mermaid sings
JournalBehaviours you watch happen are written into the journal; photo mode saves pictures there too. A rare visitor comes when the dragon is happy and there is an ember rock…
