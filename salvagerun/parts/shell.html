You're the gunner on a junky salvage tug. Gus flies. You shoot. The bank gets paid.

HOW TO PLAY

The tug Second Mortgage flies a fixed route and you only aim and shoot. The campaign has four levels, each ending with a boss: the Corvina run, Kessler Breaker Yard, the Comet Tail and the Nest.

Hangar: every credit you score goes into the wallet (half if the tug is lost, that's the insurance). Between levels, spend it on faster and harder-hitting guns, more locks, shaped warheads, hull plating, a deflector shield and a stronger tractor beam.

Fire modes: buy the scatter gun (close swarms), the cutting beam (melts one target, overheats after 3 s) and the flak cannon (shells burst beside targets), then switch between them during a level.

Debris and rocks in the lane damage the hull. Red brackets mark anything on a collision course. Big rocks and ice split into smaller pieces.

Salvage: crack open cargo pods, dead drones, engine cores, ore-streaked rocks and frozen pods. Their contents fly to the tug as credits. Green crates patch the hull. Engine cores explode and take nearby junk with them.

Lock-on volley: hold the lock button and sweep the reticle across up to 8 targets, then let go to launch homing missiles. The 2nd kill in a volley scores ×2, the 3rd ×3, up to ×8, and a full house of 8 pays a bonus.

Combo: kills in quick succession raise the multiplier up to ×8. Taking a hit resets it.

Hull mites ride on debris and jump at the tug. Later levels add mines, sentry guns, gas jets and eggs that hatch more mites.
