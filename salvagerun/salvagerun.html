You're the gunner on a junky salvage tug. Gus flies. You shoot. The bank gets paid.

HOW TO PLAY

The tug Second Mortgage flies a fixed route through a ship graveyard, an asteroid belt and an ice ring. You only aim and shoot.

Debris and rocks in the lane damage the hull. Red brackets mark anything on a collision course. Big rocks and ice split into smaller pieces.

Salvage: crack open cargo pods, dead drones, engine cores, ore-streaked rocks and frozen pods. Their contents fly to the tug as credits. Green crates patch the hull. Engine cores explode and take nearby junk with them.

Lock-on volley: hold the lock button and sweep the reticle across up to 8 targets, then let go to launch homing missiles. The 2nd kill in a volley scores ×2, the 3rd ×3, up to ×8, and a full house of 8 pays a bonus.

Combo: kills in quick succession raise the multiplier up to ×8. Taking a hit resets it.

Hull mites ride on debris and jump at the tug. At the end of the route something lives in the wreck of the Corvina Sunrise.
