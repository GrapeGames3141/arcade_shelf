# Doorbusters — design notes

Idea (user, 2026-11-24): door busters. Start lined up outside a big box store. Rush of carts inside,
trampling anything in the way, carts colliding, taking items. Goal: collect items and reach the checkout counter.

Answers to the pitch questions:
- Rivals: AI only (no online).
- Win condition: high score (dollars saved, checked out before close).
- Camera: third person.
- Power: muscle (no cart rocket).
- Look: cartoon.
- Items: just carts (no power-ups); the only extra move is the shove.
- One store to start.
- Controls: floating left-thumb joystick (up push, down brake, left/right steer), tap the right half of the screen to shove. Keyboard W/S/A/D + Space.

Store: BUYGANTIC superstore #0451 (an invented chain). Departments: Electronics, Toys, Kitchen, Home, a red-and-yellow Deal Zone,
10 checkout lanes (5 and 6 are express). Parking lot outside at pre-dawn with sodium lamps.

Ideas not built yet (ask before adding): more stores (electronics megastore, toy store, grocery, mall), rival bios/taunts per character,
a "self-checkout" lane that freezes, a store map, carrying a second basket, slow-motion on big spills.
